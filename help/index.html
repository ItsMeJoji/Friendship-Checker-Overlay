Friendship Checker Overlay Help

Connect your Twitch account and launch the overlay.

Use this page when you need a quick setup guide for the overlay. It explains how to point the app at your Twitch channel, authorize the account, and use the final URL in OBS or any browser source.

1. Connect & Authorize Twitch

Enter your Twitch channel name and Client ID. Authorizing once saves your credentials securely to your cloud profile so OBS never needs complex tokens.

2. One-Time Authorization

Clicking Authorize with Twitch will open Twitch to approve access for reading chat and channel point redemptions.

Once approved, your credentials are automatically synchronized to your cloud configuration. You do not need to copy any tokens into OBS!

3. Use the Clean OBS Overlay URL

Paste the generated clean URL into OBS as a Browser Source (width: 1920, height: 1080).

Simple Flow: 1. Enter your Twitch channel name and Client ID above. 2. Click "Authorize with Twitch" to link your channel. 3. Copy the Clean OBS URL (e.g., https://your-site/parade/?username=your_channel). 4. Paste it into an OBS Browser Source. It will automatically load your credentials from the cloud!

What the account needs

The Twitch account should be the broadcaster account for the channel you are connecting. That account must allow the overlay to read chat and listen for channel point redemptions.

  • Broadcaster login: use the account tied to the live channel.
  • Correct Client ID: this must match the Twitch app you created.
  • Cloud Sync: credentials load automatically without manual token copying.

Troubleshooting

  • Blank overlay: double-check the `username` in your OBS URL.
  • Expired tokens: if redemptions stop responding, click "Authorize with Twitch" here to refresh your cloud credentials.
  • No redemptions: confirm the channel point reward exists and matches the expected title in the overlay code.
  • Wrong channel: make sure the Twitch account and channel name belong to the same broadcaster.
